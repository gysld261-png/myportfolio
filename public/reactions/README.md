# Glass and water photographic plate

`glass-water-photo-v2.png` was generated with the built-in image generation tool (imagegen skill) for this portfolio. It is a 1254 × 1254 PNG. The original generated file is preserved in the Codex generated-images directory.

The transition combines this fixed photographic view with the existing specimen cutout, camera motion in 3D space, animated surface ripples, bubbles, and volumetric fog. The cup is a photographic plate, not a freely rotatable 3D mesh. Back and front layers are composited at runtime to place the specimen behind the rim and reflected highlights. No generated ice is substituted for the existing specimen. Main canvas resolution stays sharp; adaptive quality only changes the fog buffer.

Generation prompt: “A single extremely photorealistic studio product photograph of a plain clear thin-walled cylindrical drinking glass, filled sixty percent with perfectly clear water. Clean plate: no ice, smoke, fog, steam, splashes or hands. Square composition on a seamless nearly black charcoal backdrop RGB 11,13,14. Entire glass centered, vertical, viewed slightly from above through a 100mm product lens, everything sharp. Fine polished rim, slightly thick base, realistic refraction, narrow luminous contours, subtle irregular diffused-light reflections, meniscus, tiny air bubbles and restrained condensation. Colorless transparent water; white and neutral-gray reflections; soft contact shadow. Luxury beverage photography, no illustration, cartoon, plastic, game-render look, neon, text, logos or watermark. One glass only.”

The prompt requested 2048 × 2048; the tool returned 1254 × 1254, used here without artificial upscaling.
