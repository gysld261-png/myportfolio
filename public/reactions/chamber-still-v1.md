Use case: product-mockup.
Asset type: cinematic still photograph, the quiet opening frame of a dark portfolio website's dry-ice-in-water scene. This is a full photographic environment, not a cutout.
Primary request: Create a genuinely photorealistic luxury studio photograph of one plain, thin-walled, straight-sided cylindrical drinking glass, filled to roughly two-thirds with absolutely clear, still water. No ice yet. The glass rests on a matte black surface, almost disappearing into a near-black seamless background.
Composition/framing: wide landscape 16:9 frame, ideally 2560 x 1440. Camera at a slightly elevated near-frontal angle, long 100 mm lens, very restrained perspective. The complete glass is horizontally centered in the LOWER part of the frame. Its rim is around 47% of the image height, its base around 88%; width approximately 21% of the image width. Keep the upper half almost entirely dark negative space, as an ice specimen will be animated into that space later. Plenty of dark breathing room on both sides. No visible horizon line.
Lighting/mood: exceptionally controlled low-key product photography, photographic realism, quiet and weighty. A slender neutral silver-white strip light rakes the left rim and wall; a far dimmer short highlight defines part of the right edge. Highlights vary naturally in intensity and width, never a uniform glowing outline. The middle of the glass and its front wall are transparent dark, not gray or milky. The water's upper surface is legible as a delicate elliptical meniscus and a very soft glancing reflection; its back edge can be brighter, front edge subtle. Very faint real glass refraction and an understated contact shadow anchor the base. Black background approximately #0b0d0e, neutral silver whites, no blue tint.
Materials/textures: thin polished real glass with a modest solid glass base; clear colorless water; subtle genuine optical distortion at the meniscus and bottom edge, physically plausible uneven softbox reflections. Clear sharp optics, realistic microdetail, no exaggerated surface texture.
Constraints: ONE simple drinking glass only. Both the rim and water line must read convincingly. Glass must feel like a photographed object in a dark room, not an illustration or game object. Full glass visible, no floating, no pedestal, no label, no text, no typography, no UI, no logo, no watermark. Absolutely no smoke, fog, steam, dry ice, ice cubes, bubbles, splash, luminous liquid, frosted glass, thick neon contour, bloom, lens flare, grain overlay, extra props or stylized 3D rendering. Keep the scene restrained and elegant.
